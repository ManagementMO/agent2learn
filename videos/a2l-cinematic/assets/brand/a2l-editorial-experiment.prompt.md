# A2L editorial logo experiment

Built-in image generation. Video-only exploration; not an official identity change.
The D2L wordmark was inspected on d2l.com for its confident editorial serif
character. Its artwork is not an input or traced template.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one production-ready transparent wordmark asset for Agent2Learn,
a local-first student tool that turns Waterloo LEARN course material into
inspectable local sources for a coding agent.

Design an exceptional original compact A2L typographic logo. Take loose
inspiration from the confident, scholarly, bold editorial-serif character of
D2L's real wordmark, but create distinctly original A2L letterforms and spacing.
The exact and ONLY text is "A2L" (capital A, numeral 2, capital L).

Style: contemporary editorial meets precise developer tool. Sculptural,
confident black letterforms with generous optical counters, subtly softened
joins and carefully balanced thick/thin contrast. The A has a purposeful open
counter; the flowing 2 provides movement between the architectural A and L.
Make the three characters feel designed together, not typed in a stock font.
Strong clean silhouette, extremely legible, with no fragile hairlines.

Flat black ink only. Transparent background with a real alpha channel; no
white tile, no checkerboard baked into the pixels. One isolated logo, centered,
all three characters fully visible, compact 2:1 silhouette, with modest even
clear space. No text below, no Agent2Learn subtitle, no watermark or mockup.

Do not use D2L lettering, a green underline, their exact numeral/serifs, or any
implication of endorsement. No folder, book pictogram, circle badge, rounded
square container, graduation cap, AI sparkle, gradients, chrome material,
drop-shadow, extruded 3D, outline duplicates, presentation grid, or decorative
flourishes. Deliver a polished, vector-like raster logo ready to sit on either
the light film background or be inverted to white inside a terminal.
